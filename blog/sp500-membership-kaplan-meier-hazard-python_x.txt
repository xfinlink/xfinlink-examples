The median S&P 500 membership lasts 13.5 years.

Average the companies that already left and you get 9.6. The ones still in the index are the long ones, and dropping them biases the answer down 29%.

Kaplan-Meier on 1,462 membership spells since 1957. Exit risk after year two: a flat 5% a year, whether a company has been in for five years or twenty-five.

Membership does not wear out.
