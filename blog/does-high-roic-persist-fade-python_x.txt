Sorted the 2015 S&P 500 by return on invested capital and followed the quintiles for nine years. Top quintile median: 24.5% to 15.6%. Bottom: 1.9% to 7.1%. The gap closed to 37% of what it was. 42.5% of the top quintile was still top quintile in 2024, and 15% fell all the way to the bottom.
